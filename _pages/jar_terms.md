## Giving jars away

A gift moves a jar rather than copying it: once it's given, it leaves your shelf.

- **By link,** the jar leaves your shelf when someone opens the link. The first person to open it gets it, whoever they are, so send the link only to the person you mean it for. Until it is opened you can take it back.
- **As a file,** the jar leaves your shelf as soon as you finish sharing it, whether or not anyone opens the file. Whoever has the file can open it.

Once a gift is opened or shared as a file, we cannot return it to you or remove it from the receiver's shelf. Giving by link requires iCloud and a network connection, and we cannot promise every gift arrives. Only give what you have the right to share, and don't use gifts to send anything unlawful, harassing, or that someone hasn't agreed to receive.

A time capsule stays locked until its date for whoever holds it. When you receive a jar, its lines, photos, and recordings remain the work of the person who made them.
